What is Waravel?

Waravel is a WordPress-like dashboard for Laravel. It bridges the gap between the ease of use of WordPress and the architectural solidity of the Laravel ecosystem.

It allows non-technical users to manage content, media, and settings through a familiar, friendly interface, while developers enjoy the power, security, and flexibility of a modern Laravel application.

Why Waravel?

The Best of Both Worlds

  • For Clients: A dashboard that feels like home. If they've used WordPress, they can use Waravel. No training required.
  • For Developers: No more hacking themes or fighting plugins. You build your app in standard Laravel (Routes, Controllers, Blade), and Waravel quietly handles the content management side.

Key Features

  • Native Integration: Waravel doesn't hijack your application. It lives alongside your existing code, using its own independent authentication guard.
  • Unified Media Library: A powerful drag-and-drop media manager that automatically organizes files by date and optimizes images.
  • Smart Routing: Keep your routes/web.php clean. Waravel handles dynamic pages and nested hierarchies (like /services/web-design) automatically.
