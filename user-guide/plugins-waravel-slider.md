# Waravel Slider

The **Waravel Slider** plugin allows you to create high-impact, touch-friendly image and video hero sliders for your website without writing code. Sliders can be embedded into any page, article, or widget zone using simple one-click shortcodes.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Unlimited Slides:** Add as many slides as you need with custom titles, subtitles, call-to-action buttons, and background media.
* **Direct Media Vault Integration:** Select background graphics or videos straight from your [Media Vault](media-management.md).
* **Per-Slide Visual Controls:** Set unique overlay tints, typography colors, button styles, and slide transitions per slide.
* **Touch-Enabled & Responsive:** Fully responsive touch-swipe support for mobile devices and tablets.
* **Universal Shortcode Embedding:** Embed anywhere using `[waravel-slider id="X"]`.

---

## 🎛️ Inside the Slider Studio

Access the studio from **Tools → Plugins → Waravel Slider**:

### 1. The Sliders Index
* Displays all saved sliders in a clean table with slide counts and status indicators.
* **One-Click Shortcode Copy:** Click the copy icon next to any slider's shortcode to grab `[waravel-slider id="1"]` to your clipboard instantly!

### 2. The Slide Deck Manager
* **Drag-and-Drop Reordering:** Drag slides up or down to rearrange the playback sequence effortlessly.
* **Add Slide Button:** Instantly adds a new blank slide card to your deck.

### 3. The Slide Customizer
Click on any slide card to customize its details:
* **Background Media:** Choose an image or video from your Media Vault.
* **Content Layer:** Enter a Headline, Subtitle, and Call-to-Action button label with target link.
* **Dark / Tint Overlay:** Apply a semi-transparent dark overlay (0% to 100%) to ensure text stays crystal clear over bright photography.
* **Button Aesthetics:** Choose solid, outline, or gradient button styles with custom hover effects.

### 4. Global Animation Settings
Configure slider behavior across all slides:
* **Autoplay Toggle:** Automatically advance slides after a set interval (e.g., every 5 seconds).
* **Transition Effects:** Choose between smooth slide, fade, or zoom transitions.
* **Navigation Controls:** Show or hide previous/next arrows and bottom dot pagination indicators.

---

## 📋 Embedding Your Slider on a Page

1. Copy your slider's shortcode from the index (e.g., `[waravel-slider id="1"]`).
2. Open any page in the **[Page Editor](page-editor.md)**.
3. Paste the shortcode into the text editor, or drop it into a Shortcode block inside the **[Waravel Builder](plugins-waravel-builder.md)**.
4. Save your page. Your dynamic hero slider is live!
