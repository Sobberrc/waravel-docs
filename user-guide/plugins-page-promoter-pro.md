# Page Promoter Pro

The **Pro** edition of Page Promoter transforms your marketing into an autonomous machine. By syncing with **Google Search Console (GSC)**, it identifies high-potential queries and automatically creates and deploys Google Ads campaigns.

---

## 1. GSC-Driven Ad Generation
Leverage real search data to build high-converting ads:
- **Search Query Sync**: Pulls real search queries that are already driving impressions but need more clicks.
- **Smart Ad Copy**: Automatically generates ad headlines and descriptions based on the specific GSC queries and your page content.

---

## 2. Auto-Pilot Ads Engine
Set your marketing on autopilot:
- **Hourly Scans**: The system scans your GSC performance hourly to find new promotion opportunities.
- **Auto-Deployment**: When enabled, the system can automatically create and deploy campaigns for high-potential keywords.
- **Daily Limits**: Control your costs by setting a maximum number of automated campaigns per day.

---

## 3. Integrated Campaign Builder
Orchestrate complex campaigns without leaving Waravel:
- **One-Click Deployment**: Create campaigns, ad groups, and ads in a single workflow.
- **Budget & Bidding**: Manage your daily budget and bidding strategy directly.
- **Location Targeting**: Set geographical constraints for your automated ads.

---

## 4. Performance Logging
Keep track of every automated action:
- **Auto-Pilot Logs**: Detailed history of every campaign created by the Auto-Pilot engine.
- **Status Monitoring**: Real-time feedback on the success or failure of API deployments.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Prerequisites**
> Page Promoter Pro requires the **[Standard Page Promoter](./plugins-page-promoter.md)** to be installed and active for core API connectivity.
