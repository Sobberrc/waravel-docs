# Frequently Asked Questions

This guide answers the most common questions about using and managing the Waravel platform.

---

## 1. Design & Layouts

**What is the difference between a "Layout" and a "Skin"?**
- A **Layout** (managed in the Layout Studio) controls the *structure* of your site—where your sidebar is, how many columns your footer has, and which widgets appear in the header.
- A **Skin** (managed in the Customizer) controls the *aesthetic*—the colors, fonts, and button styles applied to that layout.

**What is a "Template"?**
A **Template** is the visual style of a single page. While your "Layout" applies to the whole site, you can choose a specific "Template" (like *Full Width* or *Right Sidebar*) for individual pages.

---

## 2. AI & Content Creation

**Which AI provider is better for Page Generation?**
- **Anthropic (Claude)** is generally superior for long-form, factual content and "reading" uploaded PDFs for context.
- **OpenAI (GPT)** is excellent for creative tasks, SEO metadata, and rapid generation of shorter pages.

**Will AI-generated pages hurt my SEO?**
Not if used correctly. Waravel's **Page Generator** allows you to provide deep context via file attachments, ensuring the AI produces high-quality, factual content that provides value to your visitors.

---

## 3. Security & Maintenance

**Is the Redirect Manager secure?**
Yes. The Redirect Manager includes built-in protection against **Redirect Loops** and blocks redirects to dangerous protocols or private IP addresses. It is designed to be as safe as it is powerful.

**What are "Zombie Pages"?**
Zombie Pages are content that exists on your site but receives zero engagement from search engines. You can use the **Page Cleaner** (Pro version) to identify these pages using real Google Search Console data and prune them to improve your site's SEO health.

---

## 4. Updates & The Marketplace

**Are Marketplace purchases instant?**
Yes. As soon as you complete a purchase on the Waravel Portal, your site's **Entitlement Sync** (via webhook) instantly unlocks the plugin or theme for installation in your local dashboard.

**Can I rollback a plugin update?**
Absolutely. If a plugin update causes an issue, navigate to **Marketplace > Registry Activity** and click **"Rollback"** to instantly restore the previous version.
