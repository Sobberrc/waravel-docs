# Skin Customizer

The **Skin Customizer** is your real-time design studio. It allows you to tailor your theme's branding, color palette, typography, and responsive spacing visually—with zero coding required. Every adjustment updates instantly in a live interactive preview before you publish it to your visitors.

You can launch the customizer from **Appearance → Themes** by clicking the **Customize** button on your active theme.

---

## 🎛️ The Top Control Bar

Across the top of the Customizer, you'll find your main workflow controls:

* **Skin Selector Dropdown:** Themes in Waravel can contain multiple "Skins" (preset design packages). Switch between preset variations with a single click.
* **Protected Factory Baseline (`Default`):** The original `Default` skin is factory-locked so you always have an untampered baseline. To customize it, simply click **Clone to Edit**—Waravel creates a fresh, editable duplicate instantly!
* **Save & Publish:** Click this amber button to make your adjustments live on your public site. A pulsing dot indicates whenever you have unsaved changes.
* **Save As...:** Save your current color schemes and fonts into a brand-new skin with a custom name (e.g., *Summer Campaign* or *Dark Edition*).
* **Live Status Badge:** Displays a vibrant green **"Live on Site"** pill when the skin you are viewing is actively rendering your public domain.
* **Interface Night Mode (🌓):** Toggle the Customizer’s own dashboard between light and dark modes to reduce eye fatigue during late-night design sessions.

### 🛡️ Automatic Draft Restoration
If your internet drops, your browser crashes, or you accidentally close the tab, Waravel has your back. The next time you open the Customizer, a gentle **Restore Draft** banner appears at the top, letting you pick up right where you left off.

## 🎨 The Settings Drawer: Zone-by-Zone Design

The collapsible sidebar on the left organizes your design into logical site zones:

### 1. Brand Colors
Set your core visual identity:
* **Primary Brand Color:** Highlights buttons, links, and key badges.
* **Secondary & Accent Colors:** Sub-headings, borders, and visual accents.
* **Background Colors:** Page canvas and container tints.

### 2. Structural Zones (Header, Body, Footer)
Clicking into any zone opens three granular sub-tabs:

#### A. Styling & Backgrounds
Choose how your section background renders:
* **Solid Color:** Clean, single hex or RGB color picker.
* **Gradients:** Create sleek modern gradients by choosing two colors and dragging the angle slider (0° to 360°).
* **Background Image:** Insert any image URL from your [Media Vault](media-management.md).
* **Looping Video Background:** Paste a direct MP4 video link to render an automatic, silent background video!

#### B. Typography
Customize font weights, heading sizes, line heights, and link colors. Handy **Reset** buttons allow you to revert individual labels back to default with a single click.

#### C. Responsive Spacing (Desktop, Tablet & Mobile)
Fine-tune margins and padding with pixel-perfect control:
* **Device Switcher:** Click between the **Desktop (🖥️)**, **Tablet (📱)**, and **Mobile (📲)** icons.
* Any margin or padding adjustments you make while a device icon is selected apply **exclusively to that screen size**—ensuring your website looks stunning on smartphones and large desktop displays alike!
* **Flexible Units:** Switch between pixels (`px`), `rem`, or percentages (`%`).

## 🌟 Topbar Configuration
Need an announcement bar above your main navigation?
* Open the **Topbar** panel and flip the **Enable Topbar** toggle to ON.
* Instantly set contact phone numbers, announcement text, social links, and custom background colors independently from the main Header.

## 💻 Additional CSS (For Power Users)
Want to inject custom micro-styling?
* The **Additional CSS** panel provides a clean, syntax-highlighted code editor.
* Custom CSS rules override theme defaults automatically and reflect in the live preview in real time!
