# The Top Bar

The Top Bar provides instant access to the most critical global site actions. It is always visible at the top of your dashboard.

---

## 1. Primary Navigation
- **Waravel (Dashboard)**: Click the logo or the "Waravel" link to return to the main console home.
- **View Site**: Opens your public-facing website in a new tab for a quick visual review of your changes.

---

## 2. System Utilities
- **[Updates](topbar-updates.md)**: Access the Update Center to manage core versions, plugin updates, and beta channels.
- **[Maintenance](topbar-maintenance.md)**: Open the Maintenance Center for Cache management, Log tailing, and Core Resource extraction.
- **[Settings](topbar-settings.md)**: Configure global site layouts, metadata, and license synchronization.

---

## 3. Account Management
Located at the far right of the top bar:
- **User > Profile**: Update your administrative details, email, and password.
- **Logout**: Securely end your current session and sign out of the dashboard.
