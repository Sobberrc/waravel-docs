# Plugins (The Extensions Engine)

Plugins are the building blocks of the Waravel ecosystem. They extend the core functionality of your site, adding everything from AI-powered content generation to high-precision marketing integrations and deep database optimization.

---

## 🚀 Content & Scaling
- **[Page Generator](./plugins-page-generator.md)**: AI-driven content creator with templates, placeholders, spintax, and bulk generation support.
- **[Page Generator Pro](./plugins-page-generator-pro.md)**: Advanced GSC automation, AI clustering, and hourly auto-pilot content gap analysis.
- **[WordPress Importer](./plugins-wp-importer.md)**: Full-site migration for pages, posts, and media with persistent job tracking and live status streaming.

---

## 🛡️ Site Health & SEO
- **[Redirect Manager](./plugins-redirect-manager.md)**: Advanced URL routing (301, 302, 307, 308) with 404 monitoring and hit tracking.
- **[Page Cleaner](./plugins-page-cleaner.md)**: Automated maintenance and local database scanning with scheduled pruning tasks.
- **[Page Cleaner Pro](./plugins-page-cleaner-pro.md)**: Performance-based pruning using Google Search Console data (Clicks/Impressions).

---

## 📈 Marketing & Engagement
- **[Page Promoter](./plugins-page-promoter.md)**: Secure Google Ads API connection and connection diagnostic console.
- **[Page Promoter Pro](./plugins-page-promoter-pro.md)**: Automated ad campaign generation and deployment powered by GSC data.
- **[Contact Form](./plugins-contact-form.md)**: Professional form builder with CAPTCHA, email notifications, and internal submission management.

---

## 🎨 Visual Layouts
- **[Waravel Builder](./plugins-waravel-builder.md)**: Professional drag-and-drop architect with template library and JSON state management.
- **[Waravel Slider](./plugins-waravel-slider.md)**: Multi-device responsive carousels with advanced per-slide styling and transition controls.

---

## 3. Expanding Your Ecosystem
New tools are added to the **Marketplace** regularly.
- **One-Click Install**: Waravel handles the download, installation, and initial setup automatically.
- **Version Management**: Keep your entire suite up-to-date with one-click updates directly from the Marketplace dashboard.
