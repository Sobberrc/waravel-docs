# Tags

Tags are the primary taxonomy system in Waravel. They allow you to categorize your pages by topic, making it easy for visitors to find related content across your entire website.

---

## 1. The Tags Console

The Tags console provides a bird's-eye view of your site's topics. Each tag is displayed as a dedicated **Topic Card**.

### Card Breakdown:
- **Topic Name**: The primary label for the category.
- **Page Counter**: A grey badge in the top-right corner shows exactly how many pages are currently using this tag.
- **Description**: (If provided) A brief summary of what this tag represents.
- **Activity Log**: Displays how long ago the tag was first created.

---

## 2. Browsing and Discovery

Tags are highly interactive and designed for discovery:
- **Hover Effects**: Move your mouse over a tag card to highlight it.
- **View Pages**: Click the "View Pages" link or the tag name to open a dedicated page listing every article or post associated with that topic.
- **Site-Wide Filtering**: When a visitor clicks a tag on your public site, Waravel automatically filters the content to show only relevant results.

---

## 3. Creating Tags

While you can browse tags here, most tag creation happens directly inside the **Page Editor**:
1.  Open any page for editing.
2.  Start typing in the **Tags** box in the sidebar.
3.  If a tag doesn't exist yet, a **"Create New Tag"** button will appear instantly, allowing you to add it to your site's taxonomy without leaving the editor.

---

## 4. Automation and "See Also"

Waravel's tag engine is "Smart":
- **Related Topics**: When viewing a tag's page, the system automatically looks for "Related Tags"—other categories that frequently appear alongside the current one.
- **Slug Management**: Waravel automatically creates search-engine-friendly URLs for your tags (e.g., a tag named "Digital Marketing" will get the URL `/tag/digital-marketing`).
