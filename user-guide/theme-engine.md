# Themes (Themes Gallery)

The Theme Engine is the visual heart of Waravel. It allows you to manage the high-level designs installed on your site and serves as the gateway to the Marketplace.

---

## 1. The Themes Index

Navigate to **Themes** in the sidebar to see your gallery of available designs.

### Browsing & Activation
- **Theme Cards**: Each theme is displayed with a high-resolution **Thumbnail**, showing you exactly how the site will look.
- **Metadata**: See the theme's Name, Version, and Author at a glance.
- **Activation Buttons**: 
    - **Activate**: Instantly apply the theme to your live site.
    - **Customize**: Enter the **[Skin Customizer](skin-customizer.md)** to change colors and fonts.
    - **Layouts**: Enter the **[Layout Studio](layout-studio.md)** to manage widget zones.

### Safe Switching
- **Rollback System**: When you activate a new theme, Waravel remembers your previous layout. If you decide to deactivate, the site will safely revert to its original "App" or "Canvas" state.

---

## 2. Expanding Your Gallery

If you need a new look, use the integrated marketplace features:

- **Add via Marketplace**: This dedicated button takes you directly to the **[Marketplace](marketplace.md)**, where you can browse and download professional premium themes.
- **Entitlement Sync**: Any theme purchased on the Waravel Portal will automatically appear in your gallery once you sync your license.
