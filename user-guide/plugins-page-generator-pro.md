# PageGenerator PRO (User Guide)

**PageGenerator PRO** elevates the base generation capabilities by introducing Auto-Pilot, Google Search Console integration, and AI-driven keyword clustering. It transitions the tool from manual bulk generation to an autonomous growth engine.

## PRO Dashboard

The enhanced dashboard gives you immediate insights into content gaps and market trends.

### GSC Content Gaps
If you have connected your Google Search Console via the Integrations tab, the dashboard will automatically analyze your site's performance to find **Striking Distance Keywords**. These are search queries where your site receives impressions (you are ranking), but you don't have a dedicated page specifically targeting that exact query.
- Click **Generate** next to a gap to instantly queue a new page targeting that keyword.

### Market Trends
If GSC is not connected, or if you want external ideas, the dashboard displays trending topics based on your configured **SEO Niche** (set in Settings).

## Auto-Pilot Automation

Auto-Pilot allows you to put your content strategy on cruise control.
- **Schedules:** Define which templates run on which days of the week.
- **Quotas:** Set a maximum number of pages to generate per day (e.g., 2 pages).
- **Keyword Sources:** Auto-Pilot pulls keywords dynamically from:
  - **GSC Internal Gaps:** Auto-creates pages for queries you are already starting to rank for.
  - **Market Discovery:** Auto-creates pages for trending topics in your niche.
  - **Placeholders:** Cycles through combinations from your placeholder lists.
- **Pacing:** Automatically spaces out the generation throughout the day (Drip Feed) to simulate natural publishing activity.

## AI Keyword Clustering

Instead of generating pages for a flat list of keywords, use the **Brainstorm Cluster** feature.
- Enter a broad topic (e.g., "Plumbing Services").
- The AI will generate a semantically grouped cluster of sub-topics (e.g., "Emergency Plumbing", "Pipe Installation", "Leak Detection").
- You can review the cluster and queue the entire group for generation simultaneously, ensuring you build comprehensive topical authority.

## Settings & Integrations

- **SEO Niche:** Define your site's primary industry (e.g., "SaaS", "Local Services") to guide the Market Discovery features.
- Ensure your Google Search Console integration is active to unlock the internal gap analysis.
