# Page Generator Pro

The **Pro** edition of the Page Generator transforms your site into an autonomous content engine. It uses real performance data from **Google Search Console (GSC)** to identify content gaps and automatically fill them.

---

## 1. GSC Automation & Insights
Connect your Google Search Console account to unlock deep SEO intelligence:
- **SEO Insights**: View a dashboard of your top-performing queries and pages.
- **Content Gap Analysis**: Automatically identify high-impression keywords that your site currently lacks dedicated pages for.

---

## 2. Auto-Pilot Mode
The "set and forget" engine for modern SEO:
- **Internal Gap Scanning**: Hourly scans of your GSC data to find keywords that *should* have a page but don't.
- **External Market Scanning**: Scans for emerging trends and market research gaps.
- **Autonomous Generation**: When Auto-Pilot is enabled, Waravel will automatically draft new pages based on the identified gaps using your pre-defined SEO templates.

---

## 3. AI Brainstorming & Clustering
Use the **AI Brain** to organize your strategy:
- **Brainstorm Clusters**: Input a seed topic, and the AI will generate a cluster of related keywords and content ideas.
- **Generate Cluster**: In one click, convert an entire cluster of ideas into a scheduled generation job.

---

## 4. Professional Templates
Pro users can define specialized templates for Auto-Pilot modes:
- **Internal Gap Templates**: Tailored for capturing missed traffic from your existing search presence.
- **External Trend Templates**: Optimized for ranking on new, high-growth market trends.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Prerequisites**
> Page Generator Pro requires the **[Standard Page Generator](./plugins-page-generator.md)** to be installed and active, as it builds upon the core generation architecture.
