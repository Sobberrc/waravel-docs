# Waravel Builder

The **Waravel Builder** is our flagship drag-and-drop page creation studio. It allows business owners, designers, and marketers to design custom, mobile-responsive page layouts without writing a single line of code or editing Blade template files.

Accessible directly when editing any page in the dashboard, the Builder gives you complete creative freedom while preserving clean, optimized HTML under the hood.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Visual Drag-and-Drop Canvas:** Place sections, columns, and content blocks anywhere on the page with pixel-perfect ease.
* **Pre-Built Block Library:** Over 30 pre-designed modules—including Hero Headers, Call-to-Action banners, Pricing Tables, Testimonial Carousels, Accordions, and Icon Grids.
* **Responsive Multi-Device Preview:** Seamlessly switch between **Desktop**, **Tablet**, and **Mobile** viewports to ensure layouts look flawless across all screen sizes.
* **Template System:** Save entire page layouts as reusable templates and deploy them across your site in seconds.
* **Zero Bloat & Blazing Speed:** Unlike traditional WordPress visual builders that generate deeply nested `div` soup, Waravel Builder generates semantic, high-performance HTML that loads instantly.

## 🎨 Inside the Builder Studio

When you switch a page to Builder mode, the interface opens into a clean, full-screen creative workspace:

### 1. The Editor Canvas
The central interactive zone. You can hover over any row, column, or block to reveal quick action handles:
* **Move:** Drag to reorder sections effortlessly.
* **Duplicate:** Clone complex rows or pricing cards in one click.
* **Delete:** Remove elements cleanly without breaking surrounding layouts.

### 2. The Blocks Drawer (Left Sidebar)
Organized into intuitive categories:
* **Layouts:** Multi-column grids (1/2, 1/3, 1/4, asymmetrical columns, full-width containers).
* **Typography:** Headings, styled paragraphs, quotes, and highlighted badges.
* **Media:** Interactive image galleries, lightbox popups, video embeds, and audio players.
* **Conversion Blocks:** Primary CTA buttons, pricing cards, countdown timers, and lead forms.
* **Interactive:** Accordion FAQs, feature tabs, sliders, and progress counters.

### 3. The Visual Style Inspector
Click any block on the canvas to open the Style Inspector:
* **Colors & Gradients:** Apply brand palette colors to text, borders, and section backgrounds.
* **Spacing Sliders:** Adjust padding and margins with intuitive visual sliders.
* **Borders & Shadows:** Add rounded corners and subtle drop shadows for depth.
* **Device-Specific Overrides:** Change font size or padding specifically for mobile phones without affecting desktop displays!

### 4. The Top Toolbar
* **Device Switcher:** Instant toggles for **Desktop (🖥️)**, **Tablet (📱)**, and **Mobile (📲)** views.
* **Undo / Redo:** Full historical state tracking—easily step back if you make an accidental layout change.
* **Layer Tree Manager:** A collapsible tree view showing your document outline, making it simple to select nested elements within complex columns.
* **Save & Publish:** Amber button to push your design live immediately.

## 📋 Step-by-Step: Designing Your First Landing Page

1. In your Waravel admin, open **Content → Pages** and click **Create Page** (or open an existing page).
2. Under the title, click the **Builder** tab to open the canvas.
3. Drag a **Full-Width Section** onto the canvas.
4. From the blocks drawer, drop a **Hero Headline** and a **Call-to-Action Button** inside.
5. Drag a **3-Column Row** below the hero section, and insert an **Icon Feature Box** into each column.
6. Click any text to edit directly inline, or use the Style Inspector to pick your brand colors.
7. Click **Save Changes**. Your custom landing page is live!
