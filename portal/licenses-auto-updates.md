# Licenses & Auto-Updates

The most powerful feature of the Waravel Portal is how it seamlessly integrates with your self-hosted CMS.

## Retrieving Your License Key
1. After purchasing a Pro or Agency plan, log into the Portal.
2. Go to **My Licenses**.
3. Copy your unique API License Key.

## Connecting Your CMS
1. Inside your self-hosted Waravel installation, navigate to **Settings > System Settings**.
2. Paste your License Key into the *Ecosystem Authentication* field.
3. Click **Verify**. 

## 1-Click Auto-Updates
Once verified, your CMS will continuously ping the `waravel-portal` registry server. When a new version of a Pro plugin or Theme is released to the `waravel-packages` repository, it will immediately appear in your CMS's **Maintenance > Updates** tab. You can install these updates with a single click—no manual FTP required.
