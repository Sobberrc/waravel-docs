# Accounts & Login

The Waravel Portal requires an active account to manage your ecosystem licenses. 

## Creating an Account
You can register for a free account at `portal.waravel.com/register`. During registration, you will need to verify your email address to unlock all portal features.

## Logging In
Access your dashboard at `portal.waravel.com/login`. 

### Security & 2FA
We highly recommend enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) in your Account Settings to protect your license keys and affiliate earnings.
