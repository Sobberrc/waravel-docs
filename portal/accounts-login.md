# Accounts & Registration

The **Waravel Portal** (`portal.waravel.com`) is the centralized platform where you manage your subscription plans, active license keys, domain bindings, and automated updates.

---

## 📝 Creating an Account

Creating an account takes less than 30 seconds:

1. Visit [portal.waravel.com](https://portal.waravel.com) or select any plan on the [Pricing Table](/portal/plans-pricing).
2. If starting with the Free tier, click **Get Started Free**.
3. In the registration modal:
   * **Full Name:** Enter your name or agency name.
   * **Email Address:** Enter your business email (used for license notifications and dashboard login).
   * **Password:** Choose a secure password.
4. Click **Start Building**.

Upon submitting the form, your account is provisioned immediately, and you are logged into your personalized Portal Dashboard.

---

## 🖥️ The Portal Dashboard

Once authenticated, your dashboard provides immediate access to your ecosystem assets:

* **Active Licenses Table:** Lists every license key generated under your account, showing the plan tier, assigned domain, expiration date, and quick-copy button.
* **Plan Management:** Switch between Monthly and Annual billing cycles or upgrade tiers at any time with prorated billing.
* **Domain Bindings:** Review and update the authorized domains connected to your license keys.
* **Security & Credentials:** Manage account passwords and update administrative contact preferences.

---

## 🔑 Accessing Your License Key

Every active plan generates a unique license key formatted as `waravel_...`.

1. Go to your **Portal Dashboard**.
2. Locate the **Active Licenses** section.
3. Click the copy icon next to your license key.
4. Keep this key handy for the [License Activation Step](/portal/licenses-auto-updates).
