## 🔍 Plan Details & Breakdown

### 1. Free Tier — Quick Launches & Community
* **Cost:** $0 Free forever.
* **Best For:** Developers testing Waravel or migrating an existing WordPress site with zero initial investment.
* **Included Features:**
  * Complete Waravel core administrative engine.
  * Access to the official Free Themes catalog.
  * The full **WordPress Importer** plugin for non-destructive live database migrations.
  * The **Contact Form** plugin for client inquiries.
  * Customer support provided via email and help desk tickets.

---

### 2. Starter Plan — Growing Sites & Essential Tools
* **Cost:** $7 / month or $59 / annually (Save ~30%).
* **Best For:** Small business websites and content creators who need professional page-building and site management tools.
* **Included Features:**
  * Everything included in the Free tier.
  * **All Base Plugins Suite:**
    * **Drag & Drop Page Builder:** Design custom layouts visually without writing HTML/CSS.
    * **Redirect Manager:** Manage 301/302 redirects, handle 404 tracking, and configure custom URL patterns.
    * **Mail Tester:** Test SMTP credentials and email deliverability directly inside the dashboard.
    * **Slider Builder:** Build mobile-responsive image carousels and visual banners.
    * **Page Cleaner:** Scan database tables and clean orphaned content, revisions, and unused media references.
    * **Page Generator:** Rapid bulk page generation for localized or template-based content.
    * **Page Promoter:** On-page SEO controls, Open Graph social share tags, and structured data schemas.
    * **Page Rating:** Embed 5-star customer review widgets with verified review capture.
    * **Media Optimizer:** Real-time WebP image conversion and lossless media compression.
    * **Page Publisher:** Schedule and distribute content across multi-channel feeds.
  * Customer assistance delivered via **Live Interactive Chat**.

---

### 3. Pro Plan — Professional Developers & Serious Creators (Most Popular)
* **Cost:** $19 / month or $149 / annually (Save ~35%).
* **Best For:** Professional developers, marketing teams, and high-traffic websites that demand automation and advanced capabilities.
* **Included Features:**
  * Everything included in the Starter plan (all base plugins and free themes).
  * **Premium Themes Access:** Access the full catalog of commercial, agency-grade design themes.
  * **Pro Editions of All Plugins:** Unlocks advanced algorithms and expanded feature sets across all plugins (e.g., `MediaOptimizerPro`, `PageCleanerPro`, `PageGeneratorPro`, `PagePromoterPro`, `PageRatingPro`, `RedirectManagerPro`).
  * **Automated Task Scheduler:** Automate routine background maintenance, scheduled image compression queues, automatic database defragmentation, and hands-off SEO updates.
  * Customer assistance delivered via **Dedicated Direct Phone Support**.

---

### 4. The Suite — Enterprise Security & Agency White-Label
* **Cost:** $39 / month or $299 / annually (Save ~36%).
* **Best For:** Digital agencies, multi-client operators, and enterprises requiring maximum security and custom branding.
* **Included Features:**
  * Everything included in the Pro tier.
  * **FileGuard Security Suite (Base & Pro Editions):**
    * Real-time filesystem integrity monitoring.
    * Automated malware quarantine and signature heuristics.
    * Anti-CSRF token verification and core file tamper alerts.
  * **Full White-Label Customization:**
    * Completely replace the Waravel logo, name, copyright, and admin color palettes with your agency branding.
    * Deliver client portals branded entirely in your agency's name.
  * Customer assistance delivered via **VIP Priority Queue Phone Support** with minimal waiting times.

## 🔑 Obtaining Your License Key

1. Navigate to the pricing table on [portal.waravel.com](https://portal.waravel.com).
2. Choose your preferred billing cycle (**Monthly** or **Annual**).
3. Click **Get Started Free** (or select **Choose Plan** on a paid tier).
4. In the **Create Account** window, enter your name, email address, and a secure password, then click **Start Building**.
5. Once authenticated, you will immediately land on your **Portal Dashboard**.
6. In the **Active Licenses** table, locate your domain's generated license key and click the copy icon.

> [!TIP]
> Ready to activate your key? Proceed to the [License Activation Walkthrough](/portal/licenses-auto-updates) to connect your key via the cPanel Terminal and unlock your website dashboard.
