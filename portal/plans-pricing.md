# Plans & Pricing

Waravel offers multiple tiers to suit your needs, from a solo freelancer to a large-scale agency.

## Available Tiers

### 1. Core (Free)
Access to the basic CMS, standard Theme Engine, and free plugins from the Marketplace.

### 2. Pro Bundle
Unlocks the Pro features for all plugins (e.g., MediaOptimizerPro, PageGeneratorPro, WaravelBuilder). Perfect for serious creators who need automation and high performance.

### 3. Agency License
Allows you to use the Pro Bundle across multiple client domains. Includes priority support and white-labeling options for the dashboard.
