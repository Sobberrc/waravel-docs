# Upgrading & Payments

## How to Upgrade
1. Log into your Waravel Portal account.
2. Navigate to the **Subscriptions** tab.
3. Select the tier you wish to upgrade to (e.g., Pro Bundle).
4. Enter your payment details securely via Stripe.

## Proration
If you are upgrading mid-billing cycle, our system will automatically calculate the prorated difference so you only pay for the remaining time on the new plan.

## Invoices & Receipts
All past invoices can be downloaded directly from the **Billing History** section in your portal dashboard.
