# Affiliate Program

We believe in growing together. The Waravel Affiliate Program allows you to earn recurring commissions for every user you refer to the Portal who purchases a Pro or Agency plan.

## How to Join
1. Navigate to the **Affiliates** tab in your Portal dashboard.
2. Accept the Terms & Conditions.
3. Generate your unique referral link.

## Tracking & Payouts
- **Real-time Tracking**: Monitor clicks, signups, and conversions directly in your portal.
- **Payouts**: Commissions are calculated at the end of each month and paid out via PayPal or wire transfer (depending on your country) once you hit the minimum threshold.
