# Waravel Portal Overview

The **Waravel Portal** is your central hub for managing everything related to your CMS ecosystem. While your Waravel installation runs on your own server, the Portal connects everything together securely via the cloud.

From here, you can:
- Manage your account and security settings.
- Purchase and upgrade subscription plans.
- Retrieve license keys to enable 1-click auto-updates for your plugins and themes.
- Join the affiliate program to earn commissions on referrals.

Use the sidebar navigation to explore how to get the most out of your Portal account.
